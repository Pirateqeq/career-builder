# career-builder

A club project to build tools that help students prepare for successful careers.

---

## Research & Planning

### Goal

Identify the most significant career-related problems students face, backed by data.

### Why

We want to build tools around what students actually struggle with, not what we assume they struggle with. Starting with data keeps us focused on problems that matter and makes sure what we build is useful.

### Data Sources

- Survey
- Interviews

### To Do List

- [ ] Finalize the survey
- [ ] Circulate the survey
- [ ] Meet and discuss the project
- [ ] Determine team roles
- [ ] Figure out interviews

*More steps will be added as we plan and discuss.*

### When We're Done

We can continue when we're able to look at our data, identify the top problems, and use that data to back up our reasoning for the tool we create.
